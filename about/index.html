The team that makes “like a local” happen.

Small-group meetups in Seoul for the Korea locals actually enjoy. We plan it, run it, improve it. You just show up.

Why we started

Some of Korea’s best moments are locked behind “locals only.”

It started with a ticket we watched disappear: a Jamsil night game, sold out in seconds, on an app that wants a Korean phone number and a Korean card.

  • The best picnic spots
  • The river-delivery trick
  • The chants everyone in the stands already knows

None of it comes in English. HanBuddy unlocks it, all in English.

Run by us, every week.
